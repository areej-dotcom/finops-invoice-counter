# Privacy Policy – FinOps Invoice Counter

Last updated: October 7, 2026

## Overview

FinOps Invoice Counter is a Chrome extension designed for internal Financial Operations use. It reads payment information displayed on authorized NASA payment pages to count, track, and review invoice payment statuses.

## Data Accessed

The extension may access payment-related information displayed on NASA payment pages, including:

- Payment IDs
- Payment status
- Invoice dates
- Payment type (such as Merchant or Partner)

## Data Storage

All information collected by the extension is stored locally in the user's browser using Chrome storage.

The extension does not transmit collected payment information to an external server.

## How the Data Is Used

The information is used only to:

- Count rejected, unpaid, and under-processing invoices
- Track changes in payment status
- Identify invoices that later become paid
- Scan Partner payment records
- Prevent duplicate counting
- Generate local CSV exports for Financial Operations reporting

## Data Sharing

FinOps Invoice Counter does not sell, transfer, or share collected user data with third parties.

## Remote Code

The extension does not use or execute remotely hosted code.

## Website Access

The extension accesses only authorized NASA payment pages under:

`https://nasa.salla.sa/*`

This access is used to read payment information required for the extension's functionality. The extension does not modify payment or invoice data in NASA.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change.
